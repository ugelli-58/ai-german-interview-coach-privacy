Job-DE Coach Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

Job-DE Coach is a static pilot application for practising German job interviews. This policy explains what information the pilot handles.

Information the pilot does not request

The app does not ask for your name, email address, password, account, contacts, photos, location, advertising identifier, or payment information. It does not maintain a user profile or practice-history database.

Practice content

Your profession selection, interview questions, typed answers, and local coaching result are held in the app's current session. The static pilot does not upload them to a backend, AI provider, database, or analytics service. Closing the app ends the session.

Microphone and speech recognition

If you choose speech practice, the app requests microphone and speech-recognition permission. Audio and speech-to-text processing may be provided by Apple or Google system services according to the applicable platform provider's terms and privacy policy. The app receives the transcription for the current session and does not send it to the previous backend.

Security and limitations

The pilot is designed to minimise data collection, but no software can guarantee absolute security. Do not speak or type confidential employer, customer, health, financial, or other sensitive personal information during testing.

Children

The app is intended for job-interview practice and is not directed to children. Please do not use it to submit personal information belonging to a child.

Changes to this policy

This policy will be updated if the app later adds analytics, accounts, cloud storage, advertising, or AI services. The latest version will be published on this page.
